# MindPlay AI 🎮

## Rock Paper Scissors using Hand Gesture Recognition

MindPlay AI is a web-based Rock Paper Scissors game where the player can play using hand gestures through their webcam.

Instead of clicking Rock, Paper, or Scissors buttons, the user simply shows their hand in front of the camera. The system detects the gesture and plays against the AI.

## 🎯 How It Works

1. Open the website.
2. Allow camera permission.
3. Click **Play Round**.
4. The AI randomly selects and locks its move.
5. A countdown starts: **3 → 2 → 1 → SHOW!**
6. Show your hand gesture in front of the camera.
7. The system detects your gesture.
8. Your move and the AI's move are displayed.
9. The winner is calculated.
10. The score is updated.
11. Click **Play Next Round** to continue.

---

## ✋ Gesture Recognition

| Gesture | Move |
|---------|------|
| Closed hand | Rock |
| Open hand | Paper |
| Two fingers | Scissors |

---

## 🏆 Game Rules

The game follows the normal Rock Paper Scissors rules:

- Rock beats Scissors
- Scissors beats Paper
- Paper beats Rock
- Same move = Draw
